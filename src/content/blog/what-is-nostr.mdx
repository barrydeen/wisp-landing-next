If you've heard of Nostr, you've probably heard it described in terms that make no sense to a normal person. "It's a decentralized protocol for censorship-resistant global communication using cryptographic key pairs over a relay-based event network." Good luck taking that to dinner conversation.

Here's the simple version: Nostr is to social media what email is to your inbox. Email isn't Gmail. Email is the protocol, SMTP, that lets you send messages from Gmail to Outlook to ProtonMail to your own server. Anyone can build an email app, and they all work together because they speak the same protocol.

Nostr is like that, but for social media posts instead of emails.

## The 30-Second Explanation

Nostr stands for "Notes and Other Stuff Transmitted by Relays." It was created in 2022 by a pseudonymous developer named Fiatjaf, who wrote the spec in a single day. Former Twitter CEO Jack Dorsey donated 14 Bitcoin (about $245,000 at the time) to fund development.

The core idea is brutally simple. Instead of one company running the whole show, there are three independent layers:

- **A key pair**, your identity is a cryptographic public/private key. No one can take it from you.
- **Relays**, anyone can run a relay, which is just a server that accepts and forwards Nostr messages. Like bulletin boards in different cafes.
- **Clients**, any app that reads from and writes to relays. Wisp, Damus, Amethyst, Primal, and dozens more.

You can use any client with any relay. You can switch clients and keep your identity. You can switch relays and keep your network. Nothing is locked in.

## How Nostr Works (Still Simple)

Let's walk through it.

**Step 1: Create your identity.** Your Nostr client generates a key pair. The public key (npub) is your address. Share it like a username. The private key (nsec) is your secret. Never share it. Your client stores it locally. No server ever sees it.

**Step 2: Post something.** Your client signs your post with your private key and sends it to one or more relays. The relay stores it and shares it with other users connected to that relay.

**Step 3: Read your feed.** Your client queries the relays you're connected to for posts from people you follow. It assembles them into a feed. Because there's no central algorithm, the default is chronological.

That's it. The protocol is deliberately minimal. It doesn't define how feeds work, how profiles look, or how discovery happens. Those are left to clients and relays. This openness is why Nostr has so many different apps and why new features appear all the time, anyone can build them without asking permission.

## What Makes Nostr Special

**You own your identity.** Your npub is permanent. It can't be taken away. It can't be suspended. It can't be deleted. On every other social platform, your account is rented from the company. On Nostr, you own it.

**No company can change the rules.** Twitter can update its terms of service. Meta can change its algorithm. Bluesky can shift its moderation policy. Nostr can't do any of that because there's no company to make the decision. The protocol is open and changes only when the community agrees.

**You choose your experience.** You pick which client to use, which relays to connect to, and which algorithm (if any) to sort your feed. If you don't like your client, switch to a different one. Your followers and posts come with you.

**Zaps are built in.** Nostr has native support for Lightning Network micropayments, called zaps. You can send small amounts of Bitcoin to support creators. It's like Twitter tips, but without a payment processor taking a cut and without the regulatory overhead. Not everyone uses zaps, but they exist for those who want them.

## What Nostr Is NOT

This matters because a lot of people get the wrong idea.

**Not a website.** Nostr is a protocol. There's no nostr.com that you sign up at. You use a client app that speaks the protocol. The protocol stays the same regardless of which app you use.

**Not a company.** There is no Nostr Inc. No CEO. No board. No investors demanding growth at any cost. Fiatjaf is pseudonymous and has explicitly said they don't want to run a company.

**Not just for crypto people.** Nostr was created by a bitcoiner and Lightning zaps are a feature, but the core value, owned identity, choice of client, censorship resistance, has nothing to do with cryptocurrency. Wisp's Normie Mode defaults to fiat currency so you never have to think about Bitcoin if you don't want to.

**Not a Twitter clone.** Nostr supports all kinds of content types through [NIP](https://github.com/nostr-protocol/nips)s ([Nostr Implementation Possibilities](https://github.com/nostr-protocol/nips)). Long-form articles, audio, video, direct messages, calendar events, job listings, new types get added all the time. It's more like a protocol for social applications than a single social network.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is Nostr in simple terms?

Nostr is a protocol for social media, like how SMTP is the protocol for email. It is not an app or a company. Your identity is a cryptographic key pair (npub/nsec). Your posts are sent to relays that anyone can run. You can use any Nostr client app to read and post.

### Who created Nostr?

Nostr was created in 2022 by a pseudonymous developer named Fiatjaf. Former Twitter CEO Jack Dorsey donated 14 Bitcoin (about $245,000 at the time) to fund development.

### Is Nostr only for Bitcoin people?

No. While Nostr was created by a bitcoiner and has native Lightning Network support (zaps), the core features work fine without it. Clients like Wisp have a Normie Mode that defaults to fiat currency.

### How is Nostr different from Twitter?

Twitter is a company that controls your account and data. Nostr is a protocol where you control your identity with a cryptographic key. No one can delete your account. Your feed is chronological by default with no algorithm. There are no ads.
