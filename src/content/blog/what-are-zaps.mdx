A like costs nothing, which is exactly why it means nothing. A zap costs
something small, and that changes the signal completely.

## What a zap actually is

A zap is a Lightning payment attached to a post. You pick an amount, confirm,
and the sender's wallet pays the recipient's wallet directly.

Wisp is not in the middle of that transaction. It connects to your wallet
through Nostr Wallet Connect and never holds funds.

## Why attach money to a post

Small payments turn appreciation into something with weight behind it. A post
with a hundred zaps has a different meaning than a post with a hundred likes,
because someone gave up something real each time.

For creators it is a direct line of support that does not depend on ad revenue,
a platform payout formula, or a follower threshold.

## What it costs

Lightning fees are typically a fraction of a cent. The practical floor is set by
your wallet provider rather than by Wisp.

There is no platform cut. We do not take a percentage, because the payment never
passes through us to take a percentage of.

## Setting up a wallet

Any NWC-compatible Lightning wallet works. You connect it once, and the
connection string is stored locally on your device.

If you would rather not connect a wallet at all, everything else in Wisp works
exactly the same. Zaps are a feature, not a requirement.
